## 1. Summary

* **2 blockers** that change what "Phase 1" means (F-01) and what it depends on (F-02).
* **21 major** findings: phase labels that contradict acceptance criteria, schema bodies that do not exist yet, an ambiguous central/simulator boundary, roles that cannot author or approve content the demo needs, and API gaps the UX needs.
* **9 minor** findings (32 in total; F-32 sits in §2.2 out of numeric order).
* The specification set is internally strong on safety, immutability and No-Code boundaries. Its weak points are *sequencing* (what must exist first), *under-specified kinds*, and *a few phase labels that were not updated when ADR-0023/0024 pulled work into Phase 1*.
